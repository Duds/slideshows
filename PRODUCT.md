# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers and academics at the University of Sydney presenting at external conferences and academic venues. Primary audience members are scholars who read and contribute to journals such as *Critical Perspectives on Accounting* — a critical, interdisciplinary social science register. Audiences are highly literate, comfortable with dense argument, and attuned to institutional signals.

## Product Purpose

A set of Quarto/RevealJS and LaTeX Beamer presentation files representing University of Sydney researchers at external academic conferences. Success means slides that project institutional authority, support rigorous argumentation, and look professionally designed without drawing attention away from the research itself.

The University of Sydney is Australia's oldest university — a heritage that could produce conservative, inward-looking design. Maud's identity deliberately inverted this: the institution's depth of history becomes evidence of pioneering spirit rather than a license for staidness. Presentations in this system operate on the same logic. They are contemporary and direct precisely because the institution behind them has earned that confidence.

## Brand Personality

Pioneering, editorially confident, forward-thinking. The brand positions Australia's oldest university as its most progressive — challenging the perception that historic equals staid. USyd Ochre is an institutional declaration, not a decorating choice. The typographic approach embraces productive tension: the scholarly and the contemporary inhabit the same surface without either capitulating to the other.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate PowerPoint decks (blue gradients, SmartArt, clip art, shadow boxes)
- Conservative academic design that mistakes restraint for rigour — bland, single-typeface timidity that looks like every other university's slides
- Heavily visual science communication styles (bright palettes, icon grids, illustration-heavy layouts)
- Any design that reads as "made for a general audience" rather than an academic peer
- Institutional caution: designs that soften or qualify the brand's confidence out of risk-aversion

## Design Principles

1. **Substance before style.** Every design choice serves the argument on the slide. Decoration that does not carry meaning is removed.
2. **Heritage in tension with the present.** The brand leverages the collision of past and present: serif formality meets sans-serif precision; the oldest institution makes the boldest typographic choices. A slide should feel like a considered declaration, not a cautious report.
3. **Text-led hierarchy.** Academic audiences read dense content. Typography, scale, and weight contrast are the primary design tools.
4. **Committed colour.** The red/white/black structure is decisive, not timid. Signal Ochre appears at every structural moment with confidence; its presence is the brand.
5. **Grid discipline.** Strict alignment and consistent spacing. Precision reads as rigour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best practice — follow WCAG AA contrast ratios as a baseline. Ensure Ochre on white and white on Ochre combinations meet contrast requirements. No formal compliance target.
